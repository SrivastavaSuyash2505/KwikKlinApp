🏷️ Discount offers setup
Piece-based pricing rate matrix

Every garment's rate for every service — type in a cell, it saves automatically. New bills use these rates, never old ones.

⚖️ Weight-based (per kg) rates
📊 Cost per garment

How the industry does it: this period's expenses ÷ the effort units of everything you billed = cost per unit. Each item's cost = unit cost × its effort units (1 = one shirt, wash & iron; a silk saree dry-cleaned is about 5). Keep Expenses up to date, adjust the effort units, then apply. After your first apply, costs refresh by themselves on the 1st of every month (last 90 days) and you get a WhatsApp note with what changed. Apply saves the costs and puts the suggested rates on your rate card. Nobody — staff, manager or you — can bill below the lowest rate (cost + minimum profit); they see why.
